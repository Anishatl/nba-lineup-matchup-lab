NBA Lineup & Matchup Lab

Pick any NBA team and see its best lineups and optimal defensive matchups, built from three seasons of play-by-play and player-tracking data.

  • Lineup explorer: every 5-man combination ranked by projected net rating (RAPM)
  • Matchup planner: who should guard whom against any opponent (Hungarian algorithm)
  • Player impact: offensive and defensive ratings for every player

Code: github.com/Anishatl/nba-lineup-matchup-lab
